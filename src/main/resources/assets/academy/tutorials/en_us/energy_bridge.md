![title]
Energy Bridge

![brief]
Energy Bridges convert between Imaginary Energy (IF) and NeoForge Energy (FE/RF).

![content]
Each external energy system has two bridge directions. The input bridge accepts wired RF/FE and supplies IF to an AcademyCraft wireless node. The output bridge receives IF from a wireless node and supplies adjacent RF/FE machines or cables.

Both bridges must be connected to an AcademyCraft node through their wireless page. Place the wired energy connection against any side of the bridge.

Conversion rate: **1 IF = 4 RF/FE**. The bridge stores 2000 IF and transfers at most 100 IF/t through the wireless network.
